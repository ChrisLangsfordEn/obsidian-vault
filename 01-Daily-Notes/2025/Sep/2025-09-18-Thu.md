#### Reflections for Kopano

*I just wanted to ask if you’d share any obstacles or challenges you’ve faced in this space that you’ve managed to overcome, and how they’ve contributed to your growth in your role. I’d also really appreciate any advice you might have for someone new joining the team*

- Permanent Staff - when we started on this account the tech leads were not happy with us. They were anxious we would arrive and try change everything. They hoarded knowledge and were very secretive.
	- This took a combination of time and effort to overcome. e.g. coffee chats in-office, lunches etc. helped a lot. Also finding areas to deliver value to the broader project helped break the walls down e.g. automating menial tasks and improving things in the background.
- Capacity planning:
	- Splitting my time across various projects is not easy. I've learned that I need to be intentional and set aside time for planning and prioritization each week.
	- Learned skills to help manage my time and prioritize the right things at the right time, and delegate where possible.
- Patience:
	- As a developer, you get feedback within minutes, hours or days. As a lead, the impact of your decisions can take weeks or months before you can see a change. 
	- Also, access issues require a lot of patience!
- People:
	- Tech is easy. Development is easy. Coordinating development through others is much more difficult. Using development tasks to grow people is hard. I have learned a lot in this space about finding ways to help people grow by meeting them where they are and providing support tailored to the individual to help them grow.
