#### Empowerment Workshop

##### Set the scene
	- As a technical anchor, the role starts shifting more towards being a force multiplier throughout your team
	- Responsibilities increase - needing to empower, delegate and hold others accountable for their own growth but also to sustain pace of delivery over time when your own time is cut down
	- Manager vs Maker schedule - the only way to find balance is to empower others, or risk being forced into the manager's schedule
##### Definitions of empowerment
	- Key words Authority, decisions 
	- systematically move away from "moving tasks" to "owning tasks"

##### The Core of Empowerment
	- Means
	- Ability
	- Accountability
		- Censure 
			- 1 specific item in a discussion
			- 2 very clear on what is expected vs what happened incorrectly
			- 3 discipline
				- Must be deliberate

**Question:**
	- Which area (means, ability, accountability) here are you strong in recognizing/doing?
		versus
	- Where do you think you need to grow and improve?

##### Trust & Power
- Trust needs to be there before you can empower
- Trust & Power
- Sincere in language

1. Building personal relationship with your team (Human beings not resources)
2. Giving time and attention - give them time to talk about what they want to
	- More time, the more your intent becomes clearer
3. Passing the intent test
4. Giving up control

##### Barriers to Empowerment
1. Those being empowered
- Some people are simply not capable
- Some people cannot be trusted
- Some don't accept responsibility/accountability

Not your usual cases - people are better than you give them credit for.

2. Those doing the empowering
	- Ability
	- Willingness

**3 Rules**
- Ask for examples from the guys
- e.g. rsvp's, confirmations, time based checks, follow ups etc.

##### 5 Steps
