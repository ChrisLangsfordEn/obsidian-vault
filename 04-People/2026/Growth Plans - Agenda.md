
### Agenda (30–45 mins)

**Pre-work (required)**

- Come prepared with **2–3 skills** you want to intentionally grow this year  
    (technical, delivery, or professional).
    
- For each skill, think about:
    
    - _Why it matters_ (to you and to the team)        
    - _What “better” looks like by year-end_        
    - _How we could tell you’ve grown_ (signals or evidence)
        

---

### 1. Purpose & Expectations (5 mins)

- Align on the goal of the session:    
    - Intentional growth, not vague aspirations        
    - Fewer skills, deeper focus
        
- Clarify roles:    
    - You own the growth        
    - I support, challenge, and help remove blockers
        

---

### 2. Skills to Grow (10–15 mins)

- Walk through the skills you’ve chosen:    
    - Why each one matters now        
    - Which is the _primary_ focus for the year        
- Sanity-check scope and focus
    

---

### 3. What Growth Looks Like (10 mins)

For each skill:

- Define **concrete outcomes**, e.g.:    
    - Things you’ll be able to do independently        
    - Situations where others would rely on you        
    - Quality, speed, or impact improvements        
- Agree on **observable signals** of progress
    

---

### 4. Measurement & Feedback (5–10 mins)

- Decide how we’ll measure growth:    
    - Deliverables, ownership, peer feedback, metrics, etc.        
- Agree how often we’ll check in and adjust
    

---

### 5. Support & Next Steps (5 mins)

- What support do you need? (time, pairing, projects, training)
    
- Capture:    
    - 1–2 concrete actions to start in the next month        
    - When we’ll review progress
