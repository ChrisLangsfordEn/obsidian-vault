 
#### Empowerment != Delegation
Empowerment also pays itself forward i.e. those you empower should be able to empower others.


Definition - authority, tools, information and autonomy to make decisions & take responsibility of their work without excessive oversight

	Process of gaining authority etc.
	Shifting from passive task-executor to an active owner of their work

Leads - ensure the team has
	- the means
	- the ability
(i.e. not just drop/delegate the work to them)
AND THEN hold them accountable 
- Discipline -> Reward model


2 top things to enable true empowerment:

- Passing the intent test
- Giving up control

5 steps:
1. Identify next step
2. Teach people to take the step
3. Test ability (& means)
4. Hand over authority (means)
5. Hold people accountable
