**This document outlines the expectations for the various roles and levels at Entelect Software. It is intended to be used by individuals, mentors, Team Leads, and account leadership alongside project-specific requirements and expectations.**

While each role and project environment is different, there are common principles that guide how performance and growth are evaluated across the organisation.

#### Progression and Readiness for the Next Level

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/PerformanceReviews.aspx#progression-and-readiness-for-the-next-level "Permalink for Progression and Readiness for the Next Level")

Progression to the next level is based on consistently strong performance in the current role, alongside evidence of readiness for greater scope and responsibility. This typically means demonstrating the behaviours, judgement, and impact expected at your current level, while also showing some of the capabilities associated with the next level.

Growth is not assessed purely on tenure or title, but on demonstrated contribution over time.

#### Flexibility Across Roles and Projects

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/PerformanceReviews.aspx#flexibility-across-roles-and-projects "Permalink for Flexibility Across Roles and Projects")

Role expectations are designed to support autonomy and flexibility. People at the same level may be working on very different projects, technologies, or customer environments, and performance should be considered in context.

We aim to avoid limiting growth based on project constraints. Instead, we focus on how individuals show ownership, adaptability, and impact within the environment they are part of.

#### Contribution Beyond Project Work

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/PerformanceReviews.aspx#contribution-beyond-project-work "Permalink for Contribution Beyond Project Work")

Involvement in the broader Entelect community - such as training initiatives, mentoring, knowledge sharing, or team-building - is considered as part of overall contribution, particularly when evaluating readiness for progression.

This is not about participation for its own sake, but about the willingness to contribute to the strength of the wider organisation.

#### Core Principles Across All Roles

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/PerformanceReviews.aspx#core-principles-across-all-roles "Permalink for Core Principles Across All Roles")

Across all levels, performance is grounded in a few underlying principles:

- **Technical competence**  
    Strong performance includes demonstrating competence within your environment - regardless of whether you are working with modern stacks, legacy systems, or constrained technical contexts. Excellence is shown through how well you work within your given reality.
    
- **Ability to deliver**  
    This includes producing quality, reliable work - whether that is code, documentation, analysis, designs, or outcomes - and taking ownership of seeing work through to completion.
    
- **Attitude and willingness**  
    We value people who show initiative, take responsibility, remain open to learning, and continually seek to improve themselves and their environment.
    
- **Work ethic and ownership**  
    Progression is strongly influenced by ownership and reliability. This is less about the number of hours worked and more about follow-through, accountability, and commitment to delivering on responsibilities. Consistently leaving work incomplete without addressing commitments or communicating appropriately is seen as a lack of ownership.
