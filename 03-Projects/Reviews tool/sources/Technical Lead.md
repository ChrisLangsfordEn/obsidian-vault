A Technical Lead is a craft leader within Entelect. Their primary responsibility is to ensure the technical quality, robustness, and sustainability of solutions delivered across their projects, while actively strengthening the engineering practice within the wider organisation. This role sits at the intersection of deep technical expertise, architectural judgement, leadership influence, and customer impact.

Technical Leads are recognised experts in their chosen engineering craft, including software engineering and data engineering. Their credibility is built through hands-on experience across diverse systems, domains, and delivery environments, enabling them to make sound technical decisions, design appropriate architectures, and guide teams through complexity. They are accountable for the technical direction and quality of delivery within their projects, including solution design, implementation approach, technical standards, and alignment to industry best practices.

Beyond project delivery, Technical Leads play a critical role in shaping the engineering community at Entelect. They contribute to defining standards, evolving technical practices, growing capability, and sharing knowledge across teams. Their influence extends beyond formal authority - they lead through trust, consistency, technical credibility, and the quality of their thinking, while enabling others to do their best work.

Strong Technical Leads are calm under pressure, pragmatic in decision-making, and deliberate in how they communicate technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. They balance technical confidence with humility, remain open to learning, and actively invest in developing the capability of others. They build strong relationships with teams and customers, act as trusted technical advisors, and focus on solutions that effectively serve business objectives while remaining maintainable, secure, and scalable.

Ultimately, a Technical Lead is accountable for ensuring that the technology delivered is not only technically sound, but reliable, fit for purpose, and impactful - for the customer, the team, and the organisation.


## So what should a Technical Lead focus on?

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#so-what-should-a-technical-lead-focus-on "Permalink for So what should a Technical Lead focus on?")

When it comes to growth and assessing performance for Technical Leads at this level, we focus on three core categories which you may see are similar to our three core values. These categories reflect how an individual grows in their craft, how they show up for others, and the impact their work has beyond themselves.

Together, they provide a holistic view of performance. Strong performance does not mean perfection in all areas, but it does require consistent evidence that the individual is developing in line with the expectations of a Technical Lead.

These categories are intentionally broad. They are designed to guide reflection and discussion during reviews, rather than to act as a checklist.

#### Competence in Craft

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#competence-in-craft "Permalink for Competence in Craft")

The Technical Lead is a leadership role at Entelect; they are expected to lead and drive technical direction, knowledge-sharing, quality and delivery for their projects. This is not limited to their direct project but includes the broader influence they can exert in their environment, customer and at Entelect.

A Technical Lead has a reputation of being a technical expert in their chosen craft. This reputation has been built on seeing several systems fail, succeed, evolve, and be delivered. Their reputation and ability to make informed craft decisions is built on their exposure to a variety of system types and technologies within different domains and environments. Their skills are transferable with people, tech and processes. A Technical Lead has enough experience to architect and structure a project, which would conform to the enterprise standards and best practice, whilst mentoring and guiding others. They deal with scoping, expectation management, architecture, design, and some customer management. A Technical Lead can quickly pick up new frameworks, technologies, methodologies, and will evaluate them effectively.

People do not question a Technical Lead's intent or credibility. A Technical Lead can make good decisions without the need for full context. A Technical Lead seeks the required context. They are focused on high-level objectives and broader delivery goals. A Technical Lead is self-aware and humble about their strengths and where they need assistance. They are confident in their abilities and are assertive with their decision-making.

The Technical Lead is held accountable for the technical direction and delivery of their respective projects. They manage and prioritise tasks in a way that brings the most value. They are required to guide the team through problems and times of crisis. A Technical Lead identifies project risks and works to resolve or mitigate these risks before they impact delivery.

A Technical Lead assists in ensuring that the team follows a well-defined delivery approach to requirements, estimations, scope, task management, change management, release management, design and architecture, and quality assurance.

A Technical Lead will be supportive and will be there for the team. They put ego aside when it comes to their decisions and will understand that their point of view will not always be correct. A Technical Lead is able influence change and lead even if they do not have explicit authority.

A Technical Lead learns new technical and soft skills on their own without assistance from others. Learning these skills comes from investing time into reading up about their craft, learning on the job and networking. They look for opportunities to step out of their comfort zone and learn new aspects of their craft and ability to influence.

#### Responsibility to others 

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#responsibility-to-others "Permalink for Responsibility to others")

A Technical Lead is trusted in their team and is an anchor in their craft. The team and customer rely on their experience and insight to ensure that the right things are being done. They have vast experience and are expected to share their knowledge with others in their team and broader environment. A Technical Lead encourages a positive outlook, even in adversity. They approach conflict with the mindset of creating a win-win outcome for all parties.

A Technical Lead will contribute to their broader team by: 

- Helping to motivate the other members of the team.
    
- Contributing to the overall team identity and culture.
    
- Being aware of what teammates are doing, struggling with, and how to help them.
    
- Temporarily taking on additional responsibility to assist their Lead, Line Manager or fellow team members when required.
    
- Proactively finding ways to get involved and bring additional value.
    

Occasionally, a Technical Lead is expected to take ownership of the delivery on a stream or project, including:

- Managing the expectations of fellow team members and the customer. 
    
- Negotiating and influencing the decisions made around the requirements and scope.
    
- Taking all administrative responsibilities from the Lead or Account Manager regarding the project.
    
- Leading meetings and project delivery ceremonies.
    
- Establishing a well-defined and well understood delivery approach.
    

Technical Leads are focused on leading and driving technical decision-making within their teams. Technical Leads build up a high-level of respect from the team members. While Technical Leads may not be wholly responsible for career development, they are expected to coach and mentor people in their given craft. 

They coach and mentor through: 

- Having awareness of the team’s affairs (strengths, weaknesses, goals, concerns, tasks, workload, limits and achievements)
    
- Setting the correct expectations and then holding their team members accountable. 
    
- Enabling team members to experiment, try or step out of their comfort zone.
    
- Responsibly pushing their team members into new growth areas and knowing when this is appropriate
    
- Soliciting and providing valuable craft focused feedback for team members. 
    

A Technical Lead maintains a calm demeanour and does not let personal circumstance impact their decision-making ability, delivery or allow these to impact the team.  They are masters at communicating their thought process and decisions to varying audiences clearly. They will use the appropriate language for the audience, i.e. not be overly technical with business stakeholders.  A Technical Lead will understand when it is appropriate to concede for the better of the project, team or customer. They can negotiate and explain a way forward (priorities, decisions, definition of scope) when there are differences of opinion.

#### Impact on customer success 

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#impact-on-customer-success "Permalink for Impact on customer success")

A Technical Lead derives value from the team’s work being used in production and benefiting the customer. They understand that their job is more than just their craft contribution. They are aware of the how the teams deliverables serve the customer's needs. When there is a lack of clarity in tasks, a Technical Lead does not blame others but pursues all collaborative avenues to find it.

A Technical Lead makes a customer feel comfortable because their intent is to achieve the same goals. A Technical Lead balances deadlines, requirements, quality, best practice and deliverables in order to achieve customer success, and make the customer look good. They remove the customer anxiety and fear by explaining and managing expectations and perceptions. They provide consistent feedback loops to the customer. Wherever they can add value to a customer, they look to get involved.

Technical Leads are trusted advisors and are respected by their customers. They are proactive in building relationships with the senior members of the customer.

## How to use these categories in reviews

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#how-to-use-these-categories-in-reviews "Permalink for How to use these categories in reviews")

When responding to review questions under each category, the focus should be on observable behaviours, technical decision-making, impact, and outcomes over time.

For the Technical Lead role, “meeting expectations” means **consistently demonstrating strong technical craft leadership, sound architectural and engineering judgement, and the level of ownership** expected at this level. This includes being **accountable for the technical quality, stability, and direction of solutions on their projects, influencing effectively across teams and clients, and maintaining credibility as a trusted technical expert within the engineering community**.

“Not meeting expectations” reflects **ongoing gaps in technical leadership, delivery quality, problem-solving, influence, or professional credibility** - particularly where these gaps persist despite feedback, support, and opportunities to improve.

“Over and above expectations” describes performance where the Technical Lead **not only delivers strongly against the core expectations of the role, but extends their impact by strengthening engineering practice beyond their immediate projects**. This may include **contributing to technical standards and ways of working, mentoring and growing other engineers, shaping community initiatives, or adding value at an organisational level once core delivery responsibilities are well managed**.

The expectations outlined below are intended to illustrate what these performance levels look like in practice and to build a shared understanding of what meeting expectations means for a Technical Lead.

## What “Meeting Expectations” looks like in practice

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#what-%E2%80%9Cmeeting-expectations%E2%80%9D-looks-like-in-practice "Permalink for What “Meeting Expectations” looks like in practice")

The detailed expectations below expand on the three review categories by describing how they typically show up day-to-day through technical delivery, communication, reliability, professionalism, learning, and ownership.

They are not intended to introduce new criteria, but rather to:

- Clarify what good performance looks like at this level
    
- Make it easier to answer the longer review questions with concrete examples
    
- Provide a consistent reference point for both self-reviews and team reviews
    

Reviewers and Technical Leads should use these expectations as supporting context when assessing performance within each of the three main categories.

#### Ability & Technical Competence

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#ability-technical-competence "Permalink for Ability & Technical Competence")

The Technical Lead demonstrates a solid and pragmatic technical foundation, with the ability to quickly understand new systems, domains, and environments. They apply sound solution architecture principles, balancing technical excellence with business drivers such as budget, timelines, and risk. 

They design solutions that appropriately solve business problems by combining domain and technical knowledge, understand the strengths and limitations of relevant technology stacks, and apply industry best practices. They ensure development processes, tooling, and quality controls (such as code reviews, CI, and release management) are well defined, consistently followed, and result in reliable, maintainable systems that meet both functional and non-functional requirements.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** poor technical decisions, weak problem-solving, or lack of architectural insight result in failed deliveries, avoidable defects, or an inability to clearly articulate solutions and technologies.
    

#### Attitude, Ownership & Initiative

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#attitude%2C-ownership-initiative "Permalink for Attitude, Ownership & Initiative")

The Technical Lead brings a consistently positive, calm, and professional attitude, takes accountability for their decisions, and demonstrates reliability under pressure. They show initiative by contributing beyond their immediate responsibilities, supporting teammates during high-pressure moments, and proactively improving the team, project, or organisation. They take ownership of outcomes, contribute to shared knowledge, and focus on collective success rather than only personal deliverables.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** negative or passive attitude is evident through poor accountability, unreliability, or complaints without proposing or implementing realistic solutions.
    

#### Deadlines & Delivery Accountability

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#deadlines-delivery-accountability "Permalink for Deadlines & Delivery Accountability")

The Technical Lead takes deadlines seriously and is known for getting things done. They plan realistically, manage expectations, negotiate scope or extensions when required, and are accountable for both their own deliverables and those of team members they work with or manage. Tasks and commitments are consistently completed within agreed timelines, except in genuine extenuating circumstances.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like: d**eadlines are missed due to poor planning, lack of ownership, or failure to manage delivery risks proactively.
    

#### Service Levels & Client Focus

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#service-levels-client-focus "Permalink for Service Levels & Client Focus")

The Technical Lead consistently seeks to add value in line with Entelect’s position as a premium professional services provider. They build strong, trusted relationships with client stakeholders at multiple levels, demonstrate a deep understanding of client business objectives, and act professionally at all times. Beyond delivery, they proactively identify opportunities to improve solutions, processes, performance, or ways of working, while prioritising the client’s business outcomes over personal technical interests.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** failure to add value beyond day-to-day delivery, demonstrates unprofessional behaviour, or receives repeated negative client feedback due to poor alignment with client objectives.
    

#### Coaching & Mentorship

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#coaching-mentorship "Permalink for Coaching & Mentorship")

The Technical Lead actively grows the technical capability of their team through guidance, constructive feedback, and regular engagement. They understand the technical expectations of roles, mentor individuals effectively, and communicate progress and gaps transparently to management. They contribute to internal knowledge-sharing initiatives and support broader capability growth across the organisation.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** little to no evidence of meaningful coaching, mentorship, feedback, or involvement in developing others.
    

#### Leadership & Influence

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#leadership-influence "Permalink for Leadership & Influence")

The Technical Lead leads by example and is respected within the team, across the company, and by the client for their technical expertise and judgement. Team members approach them for guidance, and they contribute constructively to company technical initiatives and forums. They are comfortable representing the organisation and raising concerns where necessary.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** no trust or credibility built and is largely absent from leadership conversations or technical initiatives.
    

#### Communication Skills

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#communication-skills "Permalink for Communication Skills")

The Technical Lead communicates clearly, transparently, and professionally, adapting their style to suit different audiences and contexts. They actively listen, ask clarifying questions, surface concerns early, and negotiate scope or timelines when needed. Difficult topics are handled constructively, conflicts are resolved effectively, and knowledge is documented and shared appropriately.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** poor or inconsistent communication results in misunderstandings, delayed responses, or a lack of transparency with the team, client, or management.
    

#### Timekeeping & Administration

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#timekeeping-administration "Permalink for Timekeeping & Administration")

The Technical Lead manages time responsibly, avoids clock-watching, supports others when needed, and consistently meets administrative requirements. Time capturing is accurate, clear, and submitted on time, and scheduled meetings are attended reliably.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** administrative tasks, timekeeping, or meeting commitments are frequently missed or handled carelessly.
    

#### Growth & Development

[](https://entelectza.sharepoint.com/sites/entelectintranet/SitePages/TechnicalLead.aspx#growth-development "Permalink for Growth & Development")

The Technical Lead shows measurable growth over the review period, with clear progress against previously identified development areas and tangible improvement reflected in their deliverables and impact.

- **Not meeting expectations looks like:** little or no improvement in areas previously highlighted for development.
